## Core Model: $10K/Month AI UGC + Affiliate

### The 3-Step System

**Step 1: Sign up for Glitchy**
- Affiliate network: promote offers, earn commission
- No fulfillment, no customer service
- Just get clicks and get paid
- Link: https://app.glitchy.com/ref?id=3396
- **Note:** Global platform, NOT Vietnam-specific offers

**Step 2: Find your angle BEFORE picking your offer**
- Search TikTok/Reels/YouTube Shorts in your target niche
- Watch top 10 videos from last 1-3 months
- Identify: what angle, pain point, content style the algorithm rewards
- Then go to Glitchy and pick the offer that fits that angle
- **Key insight:** Sell an offer to an EXISTING audience, don't try to create an audience for your offer

**Step 3: Build a landing page (bridge page)**
- DON'T send directly to Glitchy offer page
- Build a simple landing page in between
- Speaks to the pain point, warms viewer up, then CTA to Glitchy link
- Use Lovable or any AI page builder

## Angle Research Methodology

### Step 1: Research (1-2 hours)
- Check TikTok Creative Center (ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter) → Vietnam trends
- Search keywords in niche → top 20 videos
- Read comments for purchase intent signals

### Step 2: Analyze (30 min)
- Which videos have engagement >3%?
- Comment section: "Where to buy?" = purchase intent
- How many creators already doing this product? (avoid saturation)

### Step 3: Small Test (1 week)
- Create 3-5 videos with 3-5 different angles for SAME product
- Post 48-72 hours apart
- Track: hook retention, completion rate, profile visits, link clicks
- **Only change ONE variable per test**

### Step 4: Scale (2-4 weeks)
- Double down on winning angle
- Create content calendar around winning angle
- Test variations
