# Consistent RLM Curation Workflow

The knowledge base utilizes a standardized, multi-stage pipeline for content ingestion and organization to ensure high-quality context.

The core curation flow consistently follows four sequential stages across all domains:

*   **Extraction**: Identifying key facts and information from source content.
*   **Deduplication**: Removing redundant or similar facts to maintain data integrity.
*   **Grouping**: Categorizing extracted facts by subject for structured organization.
*   **Curation**: Final structuring and formalization of the knowledge into the context tree.

This Extraction $\rightarrow$ Deduplication $\rightarrow$ Grouping $\rightarrow$ Curation pattern is applied uniformly across both `facts` and `skill_library` domains.
