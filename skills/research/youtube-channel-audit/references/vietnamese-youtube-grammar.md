# Vietnamese YouTube Channel Visual Grammar — Field Notes

Concrete observations from auditing `@VuiVe` (1.18M subs). Use these as prior for any Vietnamese edutainment / listicle channel audit, but verify each claim against the actual channel — patterns evolve.

## Title formulas (Vietnamese listicle channels)

The dominant formula on @VuiVe is `Tất cả [các] X ... trong Y phút` ("All the X in Y minutes") — 43% of titles in a 30-video sample. The runtime is ALWAYS in the title. This is a Vietnamese idiom: Vietnamese viewers expect to see "in X minutes" so they can decide whether to commit to the video while scrolling.

Other high-frequency patterns:
- `Những X ... (Phần N)` — episodic serialization, 43% — the `(Phần N)` parenthetical is the de facto "season marker"
- `Những ... nhất` — superlative suffix — 63% — `nhất` = "the most X" (-est), the most common intensifier in Vietnamese listicle titles
- `trên thế giới` / `tại Việt Nam` — geo-cultural framing — 30%

Emoji usage: 0/30 titles use emoji. Vietnamese edutainment channels tend to skip emoji; lifestyle/influencer channels use them heavily. If the channel has 0 emoji in titles, that's a "serious content" signal, not a missed opportunity.

Capitalization: sentence case, except emphasis nouns (`NỮ THẦN`, `NAM THẦN`) and the parenthetical markers.

## Thumbnail formulas

The dominant VN listicle thumbnail:
- **Dark vignette background** (navy / dark purple / near-black) with **radial glow from center**
- **Single chibi mascot** in foreground-center, larger than everything else, with a "creepy smile" or side-eye expression
- **Grid of 6–12 framed items** behind the mascot — circular frames or square polaroids
- Each item has a **tiny Vietnamese caption** under the frame
- **NO big-font text overlay on the thumbnail itself** — title lives in the title field, not on the thumb

This breaks from the English-language YouTube norm where bright yellow comic-style text is expected on every thumbnail. Vietnamese listicle channels often let the icon grid do the storytelling.

Real human faces: NEVER in thumbnails (when present, only as illustrations in the framed items). This is intentional — the mascot is the brand's "host."

## Description template (edutainment channels)

Standard skeleton in order:
1. **Affiliate/sponsor link** (one line, first)
2. **Membership CTA**: `Đăng ký hội viên tại: / @<handle>`
3. **Cross-platform links** with 📌 pin-emoji bullets:
   - Sub-channel: `@<handle>uncut`
   - Main YouTube: `/<handle>`
   - Facebook fanpage: `/<handle>official`
   - Recruitment page (if studio)
4. **ASCII-dash wrapped copyright**:
   ```
   ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   Bản quyền thuộc về <Channel>
   Copyright by <Channel>. Please do not Reup
   ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   ```
   The "Please do not Reup" line is standard on every Vietnamese edutainment channel — it's a soft deterrent against content theft from reupload channels.
5. **Partnership block** with 🔥 emoji marker:
   ```
   🔥HỢP TÁC MỜI LIÊN HỆ:
   Email: <studio-name>@gmail.com
   Zalo: <phone> (<contact person>)
   For Chinese Business: <channel>@163.com
   ```
   The Chinese-business line (163.com = NetEase email) is a strong signal of cross-border partnerships targeting the Chinese-speaking audience via YouTube.
6. **MM:SS chapters** — always present, often include "Quảng cáo <Sponsor>" as a sponsored-segment marker.

## Ecosystem signals

- **Studio vs solo creator**: presence of a "recruitment page" + multiple contact emails + a sub-channel named `<handle>Uncut` indicates a studio operation. Solo creators typically have just one email and no sub-channel.
- **Cross-border targeting**: 163.com email = targeting Chinese-speaking audience. Naver/blog/etc. = targeting Korean audience. English-language partnership email = global.
- **No TikTok / Instagram presence**: For a Vietnamese channel, this is unusual but valid — they treat YouTube as the only platform. Many edutainment channels do this because YT CPM is still high in VN.

## Playlist organization

Vietnamese edutainment channels typically organize playlists as:
- **Branded series** with their own name (e.g. "Fact lmao động vật", "Đơn Giản Hóa Văn Học") — these are the IP the channel is building
- **Topic buckets** with single-noun names ("Lịch sử", "Game", "Bóng đá", "Xã hội") — these are search-friendly browse paths
- **Duplicates / renamed** playlists are common — channel housekeeping artifact, not intentional

## Hit video patterns (from sample of 30)

Top views (over 500K) all shared:
- "Extreme / counter-intuitive / universally relatable" topic
- Cleaner thumbnail (less crowded, more breathing space)
- Title uses a strong superlative (`nhất`, `đen tối`, `nổi tiếng`)
- Often sponsored-style or "comprehensive list" framing (1.7M Creepypasta, 1M "quốc gia bị ghét nhất", 903K "thuyết tâm lý học")

Low views (under 200K) typically:
- Sponsored videos with off-brand thumbnails (Kojima 122K)
- Niche Vietnamese cultural topics ("Nữ thần bất tử" 200K — too local for global VN audience)
- Short-format departures ("Tam Quốc 36 kế" 38K, 8 minutes)
