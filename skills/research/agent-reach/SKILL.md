## Platform Metrics Focus

### YouTube
| Metric | What It Measures | Good Benchmark |
|--------|-----------------|----------------|
| CTR (Click-Through Rate) | Thumbnail/title effectiveness | 4-10% |
| Retention | Content quality + hook strength | 50%+ avg view duration |
| Watch time | Algorithm favor + revenue | Higher = better |
| RPM | Revenue efficiency | $1-10 depending on niche |
| Subscriber growth | Channel health | Varies by stage |

### X/Twitter
| Metric | What It Measures | Good Benchmark |
|--------|-----------------|----------------|
| Impressions | Visibility | Varies by follower count |
| Engagement rate | Content resonance | 1-5% |
| Profile visits | Brand awareness | Varies |
| Mentions | Virality | Indicates trending |

### TikTok
| Metric | What It Measures | Good Benchmark |
|--------|-----------------|----------------|
| Play count | Initial reach | Varies |
| Completion rate | Content quality | 70%+ |
| Shares | Viral potential | Higher = better |
| Comments | Engagement | 1-3% of views |

## Research Flow

### Step 1: Define the Metric(s) Needed

Clarify what Anh wants to understand:
- "Which videos are performing best?" → Compare CTR, watch time, views
- "Why did this video underperform?" → Analyze retention graph, drop-off points
- "What should I post next?" → Trend analysis + competitor benchmarking

### Step 2: Platform-Native Analytics First

**YouTube Studio:**
- Retention graph → identify drop-off points
- Traffic sources → understand discovery vs search
- CTR by video → thumbnail effectiveness

**X Analytics:**
- Tweet activity dashboard
- Impressions vs engagements
- Follower growth rate

### Step 3: Comparative Analysis

Compare metrics against:
- Channel average (self-benchmarking)
- Competitor channels (competitive benchmarking)
- Platform benchmarks (industry standards)

### Step 4: Actionable Insights

For each finding, provide:
1. **What** the data shows
2. **Why** it matters
3. **What to do** differently (specific recommendation)

---

## ⚠️ CRITICAL: Data Over Opinion

When analyzing metrics:
- Report numbers accurately (don't soften bad news)
- Compare against relevant benchmarks (not cherry-picked)
- Provide specific data points, not vague generalizations
- Flag anomalies (videos that significantly over/under-performed)

**Anh wants data-driven decisions, not reassurance.**
