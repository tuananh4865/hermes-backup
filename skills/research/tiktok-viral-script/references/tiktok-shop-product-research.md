# TikTok Shop Product Research Reference

Formerly a standalone skill. Content absorbed into `tiktok-viral-script`.

## Overview

Research products for TikTok Shop affiliate content — validate product viability, find winning products, assess competition and commission rates.

## Workflow

1. **Find product links** from Shopee, TikTok Shop, or Lazada
2. **Validate product** — check reviews, ratings, competition level
3. **Assess commission** — affiliate program commission rates
4. **Research competitor content** — what viral posts exist for this product
5. **Create script** using `tiktok-viral-script` framework

## Related Skills

- `social-media-research` — platform-native research across Reddit, YouTube, X, TikTok
- `tiktok-viral-script` — content creation (uses this research output)
