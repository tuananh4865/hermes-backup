# TikTok Algorithm May 2026 — Full Update

> Captured from evening research May 8, 2026. CRITICAL UPDATE — fundamental algorithm shift.

## The Big Shift: Commerce Signals > Entertainment Signals

**Before (2024-early 2026):** TikTok weighted by entertainment signals:
- Watch time, likes, shares, comments, saves
- Entertainment value = distribution

**NOW (May 2026):** TikTok weights commerce signals:
- Product page clicks
- Add-to-cart events
- Completed purchases
- Affiliate conversion rate

**What this means:**
| Old Tactic | Still Works? | New Tactic |
|------------|-------------|------------|
| "Mua ngay!" hook | ❌ NO | Show product in use → let viewer CHOOSE to click |
| Entertaining content = reach | ❌ NO | Commerce-active content = reach |
| Pressure/push CTA | ❌ NO | Natural "click moment" in script |
| Generic viral hooks | ❌ NO | Product-specific discovery hooks |

**The "Click Moment" Rule:**
Every script MUST have a natural moment where viewer WANTS to see the product price/detail. This generates the commerce signal TikTok now rewards.

## Creator Health Rating (CHR) — FULLY ACTIVE

**The Scoring:**
| Score | Status | Impact |
|-------|--------|--------|
| 200-1000 | 🟢 GREEN | Safe, full distribution |
| 151-199 | 🟡 ORANGE | Light restrictions |
| 1-150 | 🔴 RED | Commercial features blocked |
| 0 | ⛔ BANNED | Permanently banned |

**⚠️ CRITICAL FOR AFFILIATES:**
- If YOUR affiliate partner has RED CHR → your content goes into algorithmic DEAD ZONE
- Before promoting any product → verify the creator's CHR score
- Red CHR = no affiliate link performance regardless of content quality

**How CHR is calculated:**
- Content originality score
- Commerce signal rate (clicks, ATC, purchases per view)
- Creator-to-viewer trust score
- Return/refund rate on promoted products
- Video completion rate

## Completion Rate Bar: 70%

| Completion Rate | Distribution Level |
|----------------|-------------------|
| <50% | Very limited |
| 50-70% | Moderate |
| 70%+ | Strong, potential virality |
| 80%+ with rewatches | High virality |

**Script impact:** Every second must earn the next. Front-load the hook payoff. Don't save the best moment for the end.

## Follower-First Distribution

TikTok now tests new videos FIRST with the account's followers before broader FYP.

**Implications:**
- Followers = your warm audience = first quality signal
- If followers don't engage → video dies in cold start
- **First video with a new product should be FOR followers** (not trying to go viral)
- After followers engage → FYP opens up

**Strategy:**
1. First video: Quality content for followers, establish trust
2. Second+ videos: Can aim for broader FYP with proven product

## Original Audio Priority

| Type | Priority |
|------|----------|
| Original audio you create | ✅ HIGHEST |
| Trending sounds (low competition) | ✅ HIGH |
| Popular trending sounds (saturated) | ⚠️ MEDIUM |
| Reposted content (watermark) | ❌ REDUCED |
| AI-generated voiceovers | ❌ PENALIZED |

**What this means for scripts:**
- Record with real voice (no pure TTS)
- Use trending sounds in EARLY growth phase (low competition)
- Avoid reposted content with watermarks from IG/YT
- Original commentary over dubbed audio
