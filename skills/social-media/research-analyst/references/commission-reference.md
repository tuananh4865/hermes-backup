## Commission Math (Survival Rules)

### Rule: High-commission low-price > Low-commission high-price
```
₫20,000 × 20% = ₫4,000 earnings
₫40,000 × 8%  = ₫3,200 earnings
```
→ ₫20K item at 20% EARNS MORE than ₫40K item at 8%

### Survival Rule
**Only products with 60%+ gross margin OR 15%+ commission can survive.**

### Bundle Strategy (Beat ₫3,000/order fee)
| Format | Price | Fee % | Effective |
|--------|-------|-------|-----------|
| Single | ₫80,000 | 3.75% | Low margin |
| 5-item bundle | ₫400,000 | 0.75%/item | 5x revenue |
