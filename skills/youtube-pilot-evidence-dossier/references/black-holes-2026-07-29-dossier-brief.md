# Session example: black holes pilot dossier (2026-07-29)

## What the session looked like

- Topic handed in by user: "thiên văn - vũ trụ" → after my recommendation he picked **hố đen** as the pilot.
- Format target: YouTube long-form, 18-22 min, edutainment (Vui Vẻ style).
- Trigger that fired this skill: "**oke bắt đầu nghiên cứu thu thập bằng chứng để viết script đi**" — clear pre-write research signal.
- Skill loaded before research: `youtube-channel-audit` (provided the 5-PART script structure to design the chapter map *toward*).

## The 13-section structure that worked

```
0. Phạm vi — scope, target, myth exclusion list
1. Claim nền tảng đã xác nhận — HIGH-confidence (4 foundational claims)
2. Các loại hố đen — the "types" bucket
3. Đĩa bồi tụ và tia vật chất — accretion / jets
4. Sinh ra thế nào — formation (supernova, failed supernova)
5. Tiến gần — tidal force, spaghettification, time dilation
6. Phát hiện bằng cách nào — 4 evidence types (orbit, X-ray, lensing, GW)
7. Ảnh M87* — the iconic observation
8. Sóng hấp dẫn — GW150914 anchor
9. Điều chưa biết — open mysteries (singularity, primordial, IMBH)
10. Chapter map table — Chapter | Question | Evidence | Confidence
11. Claim cấm — 7 forbidden phrasings
12. Nguồn chính — 10 first-party URLs
13. Trạng thái + Related — completion status + wikilinks
```

Total: ~15 KB. Cut nothing about the chapter map and nothing about claim cấm.

## The claim cấm section — pattern to copy verbatim

Each entry was a **specific phrasing** the script must never contain, with the reason. Reason must be source-backed, not opinion. Examples that worked:

- ❌ "Hố đen là máy hút bụi vũ trụ" → NASA myth-busting page
- ❌ "Ảnh M87* là ảnh bên trong hố đen" → NASA explicitly says it's the shadow + accretion glow
- ❌ "Hố đen nguyên thủy đã được tìm thấy" → NASA types page says "definitive proof ... not yet"
- ❌ "Điểm kỳ dị chắc chắn là điểm vật lý có mật độ vô hạn" → NASA anatomy page says "may be ... astronomers don't know which"

Pattern: each line is **claim → counter-evidence URL**. Future writers can grep the draft for the claim phrase and immediately know why it's banned.

## What NOT to copy from this dossier

- The actual astronomy content (will be different next topic).
- The specific numerical anchors (6.5B M☉ for M87*, 36+29 → 62 M☉ for GW150914) — verify per topic.
- The specific URLs — they are valid as of 2026-07-29 but NASA/ESA URLs evolve; always re-fetch.

## What TO copy

- The 13-section structure with sections 0, 10 (chapter map), 11 (claim cấm), 12 (sources), 13 (status) as **mandatory**.
- Confidence tagging convention HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW with the rules from the SKILL.md.
- The "open mysteries" section as the source of curiosity loops for retention.
- The "claim cấm" pattern of phrasing + counter-source per line.
- The first-line of Section 0 stating: *what the dossier is NOT yet* — "Script lời thoại: CHƯA viết" was the safety rail that prevented jumping to drafting.

## Failure modes I avoided

- **Did not fabricate numerical anchors.** Spent an extra search call to confirm each distance/mass against the official NASA page.
- **Did not promote MEDIUM to HIGH.** N6946-BH1 "failed supernova" candidate is MEDIUM because NASA used "possibly" and "likely", not "confirmed".
- **Did not include 3rd-party blogs.** Only NASA / ESA / LIGO / Caltech.
- **Did not write the script.** Output the dossier + 3 next-step asks, stopped. Waited for user signal.
