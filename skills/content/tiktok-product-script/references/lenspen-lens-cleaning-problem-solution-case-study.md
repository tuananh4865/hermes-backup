## 4. Why Problem-Solution fits lens cleaning (audience mapping)

The 4-part Problem-Solution template assumes the problem is something the viewer has actually experienced. Lens cleaning passes that bar:

- **Mass-market trigger**: anyone who has used a camera, điện thoại, hoặc Pocket 3 đã từng thấy ảnh mờ và tự hỏi "sao vậy".
- **Low expertise barrier**: mình không cần giải thích coating, dispersion, hay chromatic aberration. Chỉ cần nói "lens bẩn → ảnh mờ".
- **Aspirational vs Maintenance split**: người mua camera lens = đầu tư; người mua lens cleaner = bảo trì. Cả hai đều đã có camera → đối tượng khá rõ.

ARMAF Odyssey (body mist) was a "fragrance aspirational" product. Lenspen is "maintenance utility". Cùng template, cách dùng ngôn ngữ khác nhau.

## 6. NEW PATTERN — Utility-product disclaimer (lưu ý quay)

This case surfaced a pattern not in v0.3.0 of the template:

**When a product solves a real but limited problem, the script MUST bound its promises or risk sounding like it fixes everything.**

Lens cleaning tools can:
- ✅ Remove bụi, dấu tay, vệt dầu
- ✅ Make a dirty lens clean again
- ✅ Prevent "ảnh mờ vì lens bẩn" mistakes

Lens cleaning tools CANNOT:
- ❌ Fix footage that is out of focus (lấy nét sai)
- ❌ Repair scratched lens / coating damage
- ❌ Restore a lens whose surface is physically damaged

If a script says "Lens bẩn một vệt → ảnh mờ → dùng Lenspen → ảnh nét trở lại", viewers will infer that the pen also fixes other "ảnh mờ" causes. The fix:

```
> **Lưu ý quay:** Các câu "ảnh mờ hơn", "thấy vệt khi zoom" là tình huống
> trải nghiệm trong kịch bản; không nên hứa rằng dụng cụ sẽ sửa được ảnh bị
> out nét, lens xước hoặc lỗi máy.
```

And inside the script itself, the CTA explicitly bounds the claim:

> "Không món nào biến footage out nét thành nét lại được. Nhưng nếu vấn đề chỉ là bụi, dấu tay hoặc một vệt bẩn, có sẵn đồ lau sẽ đỡ cảnh về nhà mới phát hiện ảnh mờ."

**Where the disclaimer should live**:
- Top of the .md file (one-liner so any agent editing later sees it)
- Inside the script at the moment of strongest claim (the "solution" or "proof" beat where the agent could over-promise)

**Where it generalizes**: any "tool/utility" category — cleaning products, maintenance products, productivity tools, app utilities. The Problem-Solution v0.3.0 template doesn't include this disclaimer slot by default because the original ARMAF case (fragrance aspirational) didn't need it.

## 7. Reuse rules captured from this case

- **Skip Phase 0 if wiki research already exists** — verified path: `os.listdir('wiki/projects/<project>/products/')` confirmed 5 files present before script was written. No re-research needed.
- **Cite wiki files, not raw URLs, inside the script** — the wiki already handles the citation layer. Pasting URL lists inside the script bloats it.
- **For multi-product comparisons**, present the comparison only at P3 (Solution) and P4 (Proof/Price/CTA). Don't repeat the same 5-product list in every section — once at P3 with full context, once at P4 as a quick "chọn theo tình huống" recap.
- **Keep CTA short and warm** — the lenspen scripts end with "lưu lại để lần sau chọn đúng một món" or "gửi cho người mới" — never "mua cả bộ". For accessibility-tier products (25K-89K), the CTA should be educational (where to start) not transactional.
- **Don't combine "before/after" claims with utility disclaimers** — if the script has a "lens bẩn → sạch → ảnh nét" arc, the disclaimer must come immediately. Otherwise the disclaimer feels disconnected and viewers forget.

## 10. Three-category distinction (updated understanding)

With 3 case studies now, a clearer category taxonomy is emerging:

| Category | Example | Problem anchor | "Expertise" tone | Disclaimer needed? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Aspirational** | ARMAF Odyssey body mist | Lifestyle / occasion-based ("đi gym, đi cafe, đi biển") | Tư vấn nhẹ | No (fragrance can't fail visibly) |
| **Technical-accessory** | ULANZI MA66 tripod | Workflow / professional use ("quay Pocket 3 ở sa mạc") | Authority + experience | Conditional (only if product has known limits) |
| **Utility / maintenance** | Lenspen, K&F lens pen, Zeiss wipes | Real-life pain ("ảnh mờ vì lens bẩn") | Peer-to-peer; "mình cũng từng" | **YES — almost always** (utility products fail visibly when over-promised) |

The Problem-Solution template can serve all three categories, but **only utility/maintenance products need the explicit disclaimer pattern** captured in Section 6.
