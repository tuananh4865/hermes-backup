# Gen Z Slang — May 2026 Research

> Compiled: 2026-05-13 | Source: Web research, Kaiwa blog, TCC Agency

## Hot Terms (May 2026)

### lọ — HOT 🔥🔥🔥
**Origin:** Deliberate misspelling of "lỏ" (kém chất lượng). Emerged from Độ Mixi livestreams.
**Meaning:** Something cheap/tacky/funny-fail. Ironic comedy tone.
**Variants:**
- `lỏ vãi` — emphatic "kém cỏi đỡ không được"
- `lỏd` — abbreviated variant for meme effect
- `lỏ nhẹ` — mild fail, teasing tone
- `hơi lỏ` — slight fail, not catastrophic
- `lỏ thật sự` — confirmed fail, emphatic

**Note:** ~90% of TikTok "lọ" usage = "lỏ" misspelling. ~10% = actual jar/container. Context distinguishes.

**Source:** TCC Agency (2026-05-05) — https://tcc-agency.com/lo-la-gi-tren-tiktok/

### Chằm Zn
**Meaning:** "trầm cảm" (depression) + Zn (zinc symbol) = lightweight depression mood
**Vibe:** Tired but humorous self-deprecation
**Source:** Báo Văn Hóa (2025-12)

### SÍT RỊT
**Meaning:** "secret" (English loanword)
**TikTok posts:** 135M+
**Vibe:** Laid-back, casual secret-keeping

### KHÓ QUÁ BỎ QUA
**Meaning:** "too hard, skip it"
**Vibe:** Laid-back give-up attitude, Gen Z resignation humor

### Hồng hài nhi
**Meaning:** "唐僧" (Tripitaka) character parody — referring to pretty young men
**Source:** TikTok/Threads humor culture

### Dịu Kha
**Meaning:** From TikToker — describes someone gentle/attractive but still chill
**Vibe:** Soft appeal, understated attractiveness
