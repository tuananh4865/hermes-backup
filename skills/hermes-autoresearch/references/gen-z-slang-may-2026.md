## Hot Terms (May 2026)

### kịt kin — VIRAL MAY 5 🔥🔥🔥
**Origin:** From TikToker @ltun_02 (Hơi Tinh Nghịch). Mimics polite sneeze "hắt xì nhẹ" with cute expression.
**Meaning:** Cute/silly meme expression, often with two fingers touching cheeks
**Trend:** Used by KOLs: Song Luân, Trang Pháp, bé Pam, Nhật Kim Anh, Chị Cano
**Why viral:** Easy to do, cute expression, funny content, KOL amplification, short-format TikTok perfect
**Source:** khoanhdep.com (2026-05-05)

### trình là gì mà trình ai chấm — VIRAL
**Origin:** From HIEUTHUHAI song "Trình" (released Nov 7, 2024)
**Meaning:** "What's your level to judge others?" — ironic comeback against online judgment
**Key phrases:**
- "Ối dồi ôi, trình là gì mà trình ai chấm?" — viral opening
- "Ai chấm?" → "Who has the right to judge?"
- "Trình là gì?" → "You don't even have skills"
- "Rình ai tắm" → satirical exaggeration about peeping
**Why viral:** Satirical, anti-judgment sentiment resonates with Gen Z online culture
**Source:** gak.vn (2026-05-XX)

### ối dồi ôi — "Oh my god!" expression
**Meaning:** Expression of disbelief, like "oh my god!"
**Context:** Part of trình trend, used in viral TikTok content
**Source:** trình trend, May 2026

### hài hước tưởng mình vô duyên — "funny thinking I'm being rude"
**Meaning:** Ironic compliment style — saying someone is being "rude" when they're actually complimenting
**Context:** From Vietnamese netizen culture, used on Threads/TikTok (May 2026)
**Source:** vtimes.com.au (2026-05-08)

### nếu anh có yêu nói đi ngại gì — Dance Challenge MAY 12
**Meaning:** "If you love, just say it, why be shy?" — dance challenge
**Trend:** Easy dance moves, cute/flirty vibe, trending May 12, 2026
**Creator type:** Dance creators, students, friend groups
**Source:** 35express.org (2026-05-12)

### anh đâu cần phải xin lời em, mình đâu là gì của nhau đâu — Viral Sound MAY 7
**Meaning:** "I don't need to apologize, what are we anyway?" — viral sound about ambiguous relationships
**Context:** Moody, relationship ambiguity content. "Diễn sâu" (acting deep) or meme/humor style
**Trend:** Used in situationships content — seen, not replied, partner being too friendly with others
**Why viral:** Relationship ambiguity is universal Gen Z experience, easy to remix
**Source:** 35express.org (2026-05-07)

### Cổ điển, tôn trọng — "classic, respect"
**Origin:** From streamer Killerqueen (Lê Lưu Bách Đạt), spread across social media
**Meaning:** Compliment for things that are "old but still done properly"
**Usage:** Replaces negative "xưa rồi" with positive recognition
**Source:** vtimes.com.au (2026-05-08)
