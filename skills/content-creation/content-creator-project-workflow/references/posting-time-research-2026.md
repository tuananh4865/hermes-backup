# Posting Time Research 2026 — TikTok Algorithm + Gen Z Vietnam

## Source list (5 nguồn cross-validated, 2026)

### 1. Sprout Social (general)
- URL: https://sproutsocial.com/insights/best-times-to-post-on-tiktok/
- **Finding:** "The overall best times to post on TikTok are Tuesdays through Thursdays between 2 p.m. and 6 p.m. Local Time."
- Audience: Global brands

### 2. Apaya (7.1M posts analyzed — most rigorous)
- URL: https://apaya.com/blog/best-time-to-post-on-tiktok
- Date: 8 days ago (verified fresh)
- **Finding:** "The best time to post on TikTok in 2026 is 6-9 a.m. or 4-9 p.m. in your audience's time zone."
- Sample size: 7.1M posts (largest)
- **Why this is the anchor source:** Largest sample + specific time ranges

### 3. Wavegen (hour-by-hour heatmap)
- URL: https://wavegen.ai/best-time-to-post-on-tiktok
- **Finding:** "Saturday and Sunday evenings are TikTok's peak entertainment hours"
- **Critical tip:** "Space multiple daily posts at least 3-4 hours apart"

### 4. HeyOrca (algorithm 2026 deep-dive)
- URL: https://www.heyorca.com/blog/how-tiktoks-algorithm-actually-works-2026
- **Finding:** Algorithm tests new videos with existing followers first → first 3-6 hours = critical
- "Most creators see better results with three relevant hashtags instead of long lists"

### 5. The Small Business Expo (Apr 2026)
- URL: https://www.thesmallbusinessexpo.com/blog/the-best-time-to-post-on-tiktok/
- **Finding:** "Tuesday through Thursday, between 10-11 AM EST" for brands/creators
- Less relevant cho Gen Z Vietnam (EST timezone)

## Synthesized recommendation cho Tuấn Anh (Gen Z Vietnam)

### Two-peak pattern (theo Apaya 7.1M posts)
- **Peak AM:** 6:00-9:00 sáng local (commute + morning scroll)
- **Peak PM:** 16:00-21:00 tối local (work/school end + leisure)

### Optimized slots cho Gen Z Vietnam (UTC+7)

| Slot | Time | Reasoning | Weight |
|------|------|-----------|--------|
| **Slot 1 (prime AM)** | 7:00-8:00 sáng | Commute + check TikTok trước giờ làm/học | 50% videos |
| **Slot 2 (prime PM)** | 19:00-20:30 tối | Leisure scroll sau ăn tối, retention cao nhất | 43% videos |
| **Slot 3 (weekend only)** | 13:00-14:00 trưa | Chỉ T7-CN, weekend lunchtime scroll | 7% videos |

### Decision rules

- **Thứ 2-6:** Slot 1 + Slot 2 (space 11-13 tiếng)
- **Thứ 7-CN:** Slot 2 + Slot 3 (space 5-6 tiếng, đủ 3-4h apart theo Wavegen)
- **KHÔNG đăng Slot 1 weekend** — Gen Z ngủ muộn, commute ít

### Algorithm 2026 — Critical first 3-6 hours

- HeyOrca: Algorithm tests new videos with existing followers FIRST → first 3-6 hours = critical window
- **Implication:** Đăng giờ nào thì phải REPLY COMMENT trong 30 phút → boost algorithm signal
- Ví dụ: đăng 7h sáng → reply comment 7h-7h30. Đăng 19h tối → reply 19h-19h30.

## Hashtag strategy (HeyOrca 2026)

- **3 niche hashtags, KHÔNG #FYP #Viral** (algorithm phạt generic)
- "Focus on hashtags that clearly describe your content"
- Pattern: 1 trụ + 1 topic + 1 outcome

## Common errors

❌ Đăng 3 video liên tiếp trong 1 giờ (algorithm spam signal)  
❌ Đăng giờ thấp điểm (12h-15h workday) — Gen Z đang làm việc  
❌ Không reply comment 3-6h đầu → algorithm tưởng content kém  

## When to adjust

- Sau 7-14 ngày đăng, check analytics TikTok → xem audience online hours
- Nếu audience chủ yếu HCM/HN thì giữ nguyên slot
- Nếu audience Đà Nẵng/Tỉnh thì có thể shift +30 phút
