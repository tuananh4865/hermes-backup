## Key lesson

The session DB stores sessions by their START time (`started_at`), not by when they appear in the cron session list. A session that starts at 23:01 on Jul 9 has `started_at` corresponding to Jul 9 23:01, so it appears in the Jul 9 query window, NOT the Jul 10 window (even though the cron job running at 00:00 Jul 10 can see it depending on query range).

When querying for "yesterday" (Jul 9), use:
```sql
WHERE started_at >= <jul9_start_ts> ORDER BY started_at DESC
```
