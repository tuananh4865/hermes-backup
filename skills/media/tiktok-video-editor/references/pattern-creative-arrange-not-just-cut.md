## The pattern

Default ViTikTok raw review structure:
- hook curiosity → USP claim → demo → use cases → pin/battery → accessories → CTA
- Often messy: fillers mid-flow, USP buried after demo, weak ending repeated

To make it engaging for TikTok audience, RE-ARRANGE ranges:

```
ACT 1 — HOOK (curiosity mystery)
    Source: hook sentence (e.g. "Bạn biết đây là cái gì không?...")
    Purpose: grab viewer in first 3 seconds

ACT 2 — USP (punch early)
    Source: main value claim (e.g. "25.000 bát cam lực hút")
    Purpose: deliver value prop before they scroll

ACT 3 — VERSATILITY (use cases)
    Source: use cases + accessories
    Purpose: show practical usage

ACT 4 — POWERFUL CLOSER
    Source: battery/pin detail (best punchline)
    Purpose: memorable ending quote
```

## When to apply

- Anh says "cắt ghép chỉnh sửa", "arrange thu hút", "sắp xếp lại nội dung"
- Source has a good hook buried mid-clip
- Source ending is weak (repetitive, filler-heavy)
- Default: SKIP if anh says "chỉ cut thôi" or "không sắp xếp lại"

## When NOT to apply

- Source structure already good (chronological tutorial, narrative story)
- Anh wants faithful cut (e.g. for archive/educational purpose)
- Source < 60s — too short to require arrangement

## Workflow

1. Read full transcript.md end-to-end
2. Identify hook sentence (curiosity/value proposition in first 10s)
3. Identify punchline sentences (USP claim, surprising fact, strong closer)
4. Map ranges into ACT 1-4 narrative arc
5. When unclear what anh wants → ASK with `clarify` tool (anh verbatim OK approach)

## Anti-pattern

- Apologizing for "creative liberty" too much — anh gives permission upfront
- Refusing to skip "important" parts of source — bukan cutting, ARRANGE
- Defaulting to source order because "easier to verify"
